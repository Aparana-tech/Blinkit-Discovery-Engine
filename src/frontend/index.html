AI MODELING

User Personas

3 distinct buyer personas synthesized from our negative and positive data clusters.

⏱️

The Impatient Professional

Primary Driver: Speed & Convenience

Top Friction: Late deliveries past the promised 10-minute mark.

Propensity: High willingness to pay for premium speed, but high churn risk if delayed.

🛍️

The Missing-Item Skeptic

Primary Driver: Order Accuracy & Trust

Top Friction: Receiving damaged goods or missing items in large grocery hauls.

Propensity: Low trust, relies heavily on customer support. Needs transparent refund policies.

💰

The Price-Conscious Planner

Primary Driver: Value for Money

Top Friction: High handling fees and surge pricing during peak hours.

Propensity: Compares carts with Zepto and Instamart. Highly sensitive to hidden checkout fees.
