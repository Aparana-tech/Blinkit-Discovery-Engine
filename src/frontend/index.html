STRATEGIC QUESTIONS

Project Objectives Answered

How the AI Engine addresses the core Nextleap criteria.

Why do users repeatedly buy from the same categories?

Habit & Velocity Users stick to known categories because the velocity of checkout is prioritized over casual catalog exploration.

What prevents users from exploring new categories?

UX Friction The high-density, search-first UI layout heavily favors high-intent purchasing. There are limited serendipitous discovery pathways.

How do users discover products today?

NLP Clusterer Discovery currently relies heavily on external triggers rather than in-app recommendations.

What role do habits play in shopping behavior?

Sentiment Analysis Routine purchases are deeply ingrained and actively suppress cognitive willingness to cross-shop in unfamiliar categories.

What information do users need before trying a new category?

Trust & Information Gap High-ticket items require more than just an image and price. Users repeatedly complain about missing detailed product descriptions.

What frustrations emerge repeatedly?

Top Problem Themes Missing items in delivered bags, unfair cancellation/refund policies, and unexpected delivery charges.

Which user segments are more likely to experiment?

Segment Propensity Certain demographics show higher elasticity and willingness to try adjacent categories if presented contextually.

What unmet needs emerge consistently across discussions?

Cross-Platform Validation A massive unmet need for reliable, transparent customer support.
